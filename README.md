# ADIDAS-U.S.-Sales
## Data Analytics
### Project Description

You’ve been engaged as a Data Analyst by Adidas’ U.S. Sales Division to evaluate
their sales performance and identify business opportunities. The dataset contains
detailed information on product sales, revenue, regions, retailers, and sales channels.
Your task is to clean, analyze, and visualize this dataset using Microsoft Excel,
providing actionable insights that will help management make better sales and
marketing decisions.

### Project Objectives

● Explore overall sales performance across U.S. regions.

● Identify top-performing products and underperforming categories.

● Compare revenue and units sold by retailer and sales channel.

● Analyze profit margins and pricing trends.

● Discover seasonal or regional sales patterns.

● Provide business recommendations to improve sales and profitability.

### Key Metrics

Total operating profit: 47.22M

Total units sold: 2M

Total sales: 120.17M

### Tools Used
Power BI Desktop
Data modeling with DAX measures and calculated columns
Visualizations: bar charts, line charts, stacked bar charts, and KPIs

### 📁 Deliverables
1. Excel (.xlsx) file containing cleaned data, analysis, and visual dashboards.
2. PowerPoint presentation summarizing your findings and insights.
